# Filipinohomes deep research (2026-10-06)

Source: `/deep-research` run `wf_32ec61ab-571`. 22 sources fetched, 93 claims extracted, 25 verified (3-vote adversarial), 12 confirmed, 13 killed, 68 never verified (budget cut).

**Read this first:** only the CONFIRMED section is safe to build on. UNVERIFIED is a lead list, not fact. REFUTED claims must not appear in any doc, pitch or price sheet.

## Summary

Filipinohomes (filipinohomes.com) is confirmed as a Cebu-based real-estate brand led by founder and CEO Anthony Gerard Leuterio, active as of July 2026. It presents as a hybrid: a nationwide network of property specialists (brokerage style) that also runs listings and agent-directory pages. The strongest product hook found is its own public "Inquiry Reply" metric. Even the fastest agents reply in about 10 to 48 minutes, and the list falls to hours further down, so an instant Taglish first responder has clear room to beat most agents. Viber pricing and the Facebook Lead Ads webhook path are verified and usable for MVP planning. The NPC AI advisory (2024-04) applies to a lead-qualification agent, but it sets a privacy-notice duty, not a "bot must announce it is AI" rule. Company size, competitor and pricing benchmarks, PH lead-handling norms, SMS/Meta bot policy, broker licensing and the voice-vs-text and team-split questions were NOT established by this run, so the PRD should treat them as open items. The team split is a planning decision, not something this research verified.

Do not read their absence from the confirmed list as evidence they are false or that nothing exists on them. The Viber rate card and FilBench/Batayan are the strongest sources, but the benchmarks predate current frontier models. Dates: most verified sources are 2025-2026, and the Lamudi article is undated. Product-risk flag for the PRD: the project's rule that agents must never reveal they are AI is not itself ruled out by NPC 2024-04, but the advisory's transparency duty about AI-driven processing, plus unresearched Meta/Viber platform policies, means the privacy notice, consent capture and platform bot-disclosure rules need a decision before the beta goes live with real leads. The 50/50 backend-agent vs frontend-mobile split is a planning choice for the PRD, not something the research validated.
